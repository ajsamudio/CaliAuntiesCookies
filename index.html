Our Story

A little love in every bite.

At Cali Aunties, we believe the sweetest things in life are homemade.

What started as a love for baking and sharing cookies with the people around us has grown into something a little bigger, a little bolder, and a whole lot sweeter.

Every batch is made from scratch, with carefully chosen ingredients, creative flavors, and plenty of love. Because to us, cookies aren't just a treat. They're a way to celebrate life's little moments, make someone smile, and turn an ordinary day into something special.

And if that means sneaking an extra cookie when nobody's looking? We don't judge. 😉

Questions

Ask Auntie

Don't see your question? Send us a DM on Instagram. Auntie loves to chat.

When can I place an order?+

Soon! We're waiting on our Orange County Class B Cottage Food Operation approval, and online preorders open as soon as it's official. Follow @caliauntiescookies to hear first.

What box sizes do you offer?+

Three levels of temptation: The Flirty 4-Pack ($16, one flavor), The Sweet 6-Pack ($22, one flavor) and The Party Dozen ($42, up to two flavors). Subject to availability.

Do you deliver or ship?+

Not right now. All orders are scheduled local pickups in Irvine. We'll confirm the pickup details when we reply to your request.

What about allergies?+

Our cookies are made in a home kitchen that also handles wheat, dairy, eggs, soy, peanuts and tree nuts, so we can't promise anything is allergen-free. Ask us about specific ingredients any time.

Do you do larger orders or events?+

We're starting small with boxes for local pickup, but larger orders, special occasions and business partnerships are on the way. DM us on Instagram if you've got something in mind.
