Our Story

Every family has that auntie.

The one who shows up with a tin of warm cookies, a hug that lasts a little too long, and opinions about your love life. That's us.

Cali Aunties started in a home kitchen with a family recipe, a hand mixer that has seen things, and one simple belief: happiness is homemade. Every cookie is mixed, scooped and baked by hand in small batches, so it tastes like it came from someone who loves you. Because it did.

xoxo, Auntie

Small batch, big love

No factories, no shortcuts. Just a few dozen at a time, made with the kind of attention you can taste.

Real ingredients

Real butter, real vanilla, real chocolate. If Auntie can't pronounce it, it's not going in the bowl.

Baked to order

Nothing sits on a shelf. Your cookies go in the oven just for you, so they show up tasting like they should.

Questions

Ask Auntie

Don't see your question? Just ask in your inquiry. Auntie loves to chat.

When can I place an order?+

We're putting the finishing touches on our licensing right now. Send an inquiry today and you'll be first in line the moment the ovens officially open.

How much notice do you need?+

Everything is baked to order, so 3–5 days is ideal for regular orders. For big events like weddings, showers or parties, give us about two weeks.

Do you deliver?+

Local pickup is our go-to, and delivery may be available in select nearby areas. We'll confirm the details when we reply to your inquiry.

What about allergies?+

Our cookies are made in a home kitchen that also handles wheat, dairy, eggs, soy, peanuts and tree nuts, so we can't promise anything is allergen-free. Ask us about specific ingredients any time.

Can I get a custom flavor or party box?+

Yes, please! Custom flavors, party favors, gift boxes, themed colors: tell us what you're dreaming of and we'll make it happen.
